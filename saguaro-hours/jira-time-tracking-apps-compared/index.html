Jira time tracking apps compared

Five Jira Cloud time tracking apps from the Atlassian Marketplace, plus mine, Saguaro Hours for Jira. Prices come from each app's public Marketplace price list. Features come from each listing, the vendor's own pages, or my notes where marked. This is not a review of how well any app works; I have not used them on a real team.

Saguaro Hours is not the cheapest app here. It costs less than Tempo and more than the other four. The table shows by how much.

Who should pick another app

  • You want the lowest price and mainly need reports. SolDevelo Worklogs and Clockwork Lite list at $40.00 a month for 100 users, a fifth of Saguaro's $200.00. SolDevelo also keeps its data inside Atlassian.
  • You want approvals, billing and timers for less than Saguaro. Clockwork Pro lists approvals with locked billing periods, rates and costs, automatic timers, calendar sync and an API, at $130.00 a month for 100 users.
  • You want timers, accounts and an API, and do not need approvals. Timetracker lists all three at $100.00 a month for 100 users.
  • You need planning, accounts, many integrations, an API or certifications. Tempo and its companion apps cover them. Saguaro Hours has no API, no Slack or mobile app, no planning and no certifications.
  • You want an app with a track record. Saguaro Hours is new. It has no installs or reviews yet, and one person (me) builds and supports it.

Who Saguaro Hours is for

Teams that need a lead to sign off time, and want the data to stay in Atlassian:

  • Weekly or monthly periods per team; submit, approve, reject with a comment, approve all; a backup approver; an option that nobody approves their own timesheet.
  • Approved days lock in the app, and a later edit made in Jira itself is flagged on the sheet with the hours before and after.
  • Required hours per person, holidays and days off, so part-timers are not shown as short.
  • A billable flag per worklog, rates with start dates and a printable billing summary.
  • Reports by any Jira field, with a JQL filter and estimate vs logged; CSV and Excel export.
  • Worklogs are normal Jira worklogs written as the signed-in user; if you uninstall, they stay in Jira.
  • Moving from Tempo: the import keeps billable flags, accounts, work attributes and who logged each worklog.

Status: Saguaro Hours was submitted to the Atlassian Marketplace on 2026-11-03 and is waiting for approval. The features above are built and tested on my development site as of 2026-11-04. The build under review is older: it has the weekly grid, the Log work panel, billable flags and attributes, teams with a lead, one period type for the site, submit and approve, locks with after-lock flags, reports, exports, the gadget and the Tempo import. It does not yet have the timer, rates and the billing summary, backup approvers, per-team periods, per-person hours, reports by Jira field or JQL, email reminders or the CSV and calendar import.
