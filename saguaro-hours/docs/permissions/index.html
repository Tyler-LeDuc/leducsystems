Permissions

What the app asks Jira for

Scope Why
read:jira-work Read issues and worklogs, run searches for your week and for reports, and receive worklog events.
write:jira-work Create, edit and delete your worklogs when you use the grid, the panel, the timer or the Import tab. If an administrator turns on email reminders, also send them through Jira's own mail (Jira's "Send notification for issue", which needs this scope).
read:jira-user Look up your account and time zone, and search for users when an admin builds a team.
storage:app Store billable flags, attributes, settings (including holidays and timer rounding), teams, timesheet records, saved reports, people's working hours, timers, hourly rates and each person's remembered calendar event titles in Atlassian's Forge storage.
report:personal-data Report the account IDs the app stores to Atlassian once a week, as Atlassian requires, so the app can erase people whose accounts were closed and update changed names.

Worklog calls from the grid, the panel, the timer and the Import tab run as the signed-in user, so Jira's own permissions apply. Email reminders are sent as the app, on the reminder issue the administrator picks, and Jira sends them only to people who can browse that issue.

Who can do what

Action Who
Log time, submit own timesheet, run and save reports Any user
Import time from a worklog CSV or a calendar file Any user, for their own time only: every imported worklog is written as them, and rows naming someone else are refused. Nobody, administrators included, can import another person's time
Start, pause, stop, log and discard a timer Any user, on issues they can browse, for their own timers only
See rates, cost and revenue in reports and the billing summary Jira administrators and the people an administrator lists under "Who can see money". Not team leads by default. Everyone else never receives rates or amounts from the app
Set billable and attributes on a worklog The worklog's author
Review a team's timesheets, approve, reject The team's lead or backup approver, or a Jira administrator for any team. With "Nobody approves their own timesheet" on, not for one's own sheet
See the dashboard gadget for a team Team members, the lead, the backup approver, and Jira administrators
Admin page: settings, holidays, timer rounding, working hours, rates and who can see money, attributes, teams, email reminders (including the test email and the preview), Tempo import, data export Jira administrators only (checked with Jira's Administer Jira permission on each call)
See a person's required hours The person, the leads and backup approvers of their teams (Approvals, reports), their team (the gadget, totals only), and Jira administrators. Days off dates: Jira administrators only (admin page), and the person in their own grid

There are no separate app roles beyond one list. The roles are Jira administrator, team lead, backup approver, rate viewer (the "Who can see money" list) and everyone else.

A team lead or backup approver sees only their own teams in Approvals. They cannot review another team, or a person outside their team.

What a user can see

Reports and the grid use native Jira reads as the viewer. A user sees worklogs on issues they can browse. The billable flag and attributes of a worklog are shown only to people Jira lets see that worklog. The real author a Tempo import stored for a worklog is shown with that worklog's hours, also only to people Jira lets see the worklog. The imported description is never shown on these screens.

When the subscription is not active

The timesheet page, the panel, the gadget and the admin page show a message instead of data. Jira administrators can still download all app data from the admin page. The app keeps its stored data in step with worklog changes made in Jira, so the data is correct if the subscription returns.

Network

The app makes no calls to servers outside Atlassian. Files you import are read in your browser, and exports are built in your browser.
