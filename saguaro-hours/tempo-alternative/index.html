A Tempo alternative for Jira Cloud

If Tempo Timesheets costs more than your team wants to pay, Saguaro Hours for Jira is one option. It does weekly timesheets, team approvals and billable reports at a lower list price. It also does fewer things than Tempo. This page shows the prices, with sources, and the features Saguaro does not have.

If you rely on Tempo accounts, capacity planning, integrations, its API, or its mobile and Slack apps, Saguaro Hours is not a replacement today.

What Saguaro Hours does differently

  • Worklogs are normal Jira worklogs, written as the signed-in user. Jira's Work log tab and worklogAuthor searches show them. If you uninstall Saguaro, the worklogs stay in Jira.
  • It is built on Atlassian Forge. Its data is kept in Atlassian-hosted storage, and the app declares no outside network access.
  • It is free for up to 10 users, with no flat fee.

Moving from Tempo

See your own Tempo export as Saguaro Hours timesheets, in your browser: Tempo data preview.

Your worklogs are already in Jira, because Tempo writes them as Jira worklogs. Saguaro Hours adds what Tempo kept on top: export Tempo's Logged Time report as CSV (Tempo > Reports > Logged Time > Export > Download Raw Data > CSV) and import it on the admin page. It matches each row to its Jira worklog and keeps billable flags, accounts and up to five work attributes. It also keeps who logged each worklog in Tempo, so reports, approvals and timesheets count migrated hours for that person, marked "from Tempo" (in Jira itself the Tempo app stays the author). It was tested on a real Tempo export, and running it twice changes nothing. Step by step: move from Tempo guide.
