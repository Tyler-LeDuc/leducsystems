Tempo Timesheets price increase 2026: what it costs your team

Tempo raised its list prices on the Atlassian Marketplace on September 15, 2026. Tempo's announcement puts Timesheets in the group that went up 13 to 15% on Jira Cloud. Existing customers can renew at the old price until November 14, 2026, if they keep the same user tier. After that date, renewals move to the new price.

This page shows the old and new rates, works out what the change means for your user count, and compares both with Saguaro Hours for Jira, the time tracking app I make. Read the comparison with that in mind.

Should you renew Tempo before November 14?

If you are staying on Tempo, renewing before the deadline is the cheaper choice. Tempo's announcement says existing customers can renew at the old price through November 14, 2026 as long as the user tier stays the same. Renewals after that date are at the new price. If you have a quote from Tempo or a partner, the quoted price holds until the quote expires.

Tempo also warns that some quote or subscription changes can trigger a recalculation of the price, and says to check with your channel partner or account manager. Changing your user tier is one of those cases.

If the price is why you are looking around, compare the whole bill, not only the increase. The new price at 100 users is $599.00 a month. The same 100 users on Saguaro Hours list at $200.00 a month.

How Saguaro Hours is priced
