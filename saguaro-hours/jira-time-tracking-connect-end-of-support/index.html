Connect end of support and Jira time tracking apps

Atlassian is ending support for Connect, the older way of building Jira Cloud apps. This page sets out the dates, what Atlassian says end of support means, which Jira time tracking apps the Atlassian Marketplace currently lists as Connect apps, and how to check an app yourself. I make Saguaro Hours, a time tracking app built on Forge, so read the last section with that in mind.

The dates

From Atlassian's announcement, "Announcing Connect End of Support: Timeline and Next Steps" (published 2025-03-17, https://www.atlassian.com/blog/developer/announcing-connect-end-of-support-timeline-and-next-steps ):

  • September 17, 2025: new Marketplace apps must be built on Forge.
  • March 2026: "Marketplace apps with a Connect descriptor can no longer be updated." Apps that have adopted the Forge manifest can still receive updates.
  • End of support, first planned for Q4 2026. On 2026-08-03 Atlassian moved it: "We're extending the Connect end of support date from December 2026 to January 31, 2027" (https://community.developer.atlassian.com/t/connect-end-of-support-extended-to-january-31-2027/102002 ).

An app with a Forge manifest that still contains Connect modules is "considered a Connect app until all Connect modules are removed", according to the same announcement.

What end of support means for an installed app

Atlassian's announcement describes it as "use at your own risk": Connect gets no new features and only critical or security bugs are fixed. Customers keep access to Connect apps they have installed, but Atlassian calls that an undesired state because of the lack of support. All quotes and dates here were read on 2026-11-04.

So nothing is switched off on January 31, 2027 by that announcement alone. What changes is the support behind the app, and the vendor's ability to ship a fixed version on Connect, unless the app has adopted the Forge manifest.

How to check any app yourself

Open this address in a browser, with the app's key in place of KEY:

<https://marketplace.atlassian.com/rest/2/addons/KEY/versions/latest?hosting=cloud>

In the answer, "deployment" has "connect": true for a Connect app and "connect": false otherwise, and "release" has the date. To find an app's key, search by name at <https://marketplace.atlassian.com/rest/2/addons?hosting=cloud&text=NAME> and read the "key" field. For example, Clockify's key is clockify-timesheets-time-tracking-reports.

If you decide to move

  • Look at where your time actually lives. If the app writes Jira worklogs, those stay in Jira when the app is removed, and any other time tracking app that reads Jira worklogs sees them. Time kept only in the vendor's own service stays there; export it from that service first.
  • Export before you uninstall.
  • Check the replacement's platform with the address above, so you only move once.
