Saguaro Hours for Jira

Weekly timesheets, lead approval and billable reports for Jira Cloud.

It is for teams that log time in Jira and need three things Jira does not give them: a weekly timesheet per person, a team lead who signs it off, and a report of billable hours.

Price: free for up to 10 users. Above 10 users, each band of users has its own monthly price: $2.00 per user for users 1 to 100, $1.50 for users 101 to 250, and $1.00 for users 251 to 1,000. Billing goes through Atlassian.

Status: submitted to the Atlassian Marketplace on 2026-11-03 and waiting for Atlassian's approval. To hear the day it is live, email tyler@leducsystems.com with the subject "Saguaro launch".

What it does

A weekly grid that writes normal Jira worklogs

Rows are issues and columns are days. Type 1.5, 1:30, 1h 30m or 90m in a cell. Each entry is a normal Jira worklog written as the signed-in user, so Jira's Work log tab and worklogAuthor searches keep working. Copy last week adds last week's rows, without hours. A chip shows the hours still short of the required hours.

Log work from the issue

A Log work panel on each issue has the date, time spent, a billable box, your own attributes and a description. Your entries on that issue are listed under the form.

Submit a week or a month

The admin picks weekly or monthly periods for the site. Staff submit the period when it is done. A submitted period is read-only in the app. It stays locked once approved, and a rejection opens it again. A banner points to earlier periods that are short of the required hours.

Lead approval with an audit trail

Team leads approve a sheet, reject it with a comment, or approve every submitted sheet in the team at once. Approved days stay locked in the app. If someone edits a locked day in Jira itself, the sheet is flagged with the hours before and after. Each step goes into an audit trail.

Billable reports with CSV and Excel export

Group hours by user, team, project, epic, issue type, label, billable or any attribute you define, with a second grouping if you want one. Save a report to run again. Export Summary CSV, Detail CSV or Excel (.xlsx).

A dashboard gadget per team

The gadget shows the team's hours this period, who has not submitted, and how many approvals are waiting.

Admin settings and teams

A Jira administrator sets the period type, the required hours per week, the billable default and custom attributes, then builds teams with a lead and members. The admin page can also download all data the app stores.

Your data stays in Atlassian

  • Saguaro Hours is built on Atlassian Forge. Atlassian hosts its code, and its data is kept in Atlassian-hosted storage.
  • The app declares no outside network access, so it makes no calls to outside servers.
  • Worklogs are stored by Jira, not by the app. If you uninstall, your worklogs stay in Jira.
  • Details: Security and Privacy.

Not in Saguaro Hours

Capacity planning, invoices, calendar sync, a public API, and Slack or mobile apps are not part of Saguaro Hours.
