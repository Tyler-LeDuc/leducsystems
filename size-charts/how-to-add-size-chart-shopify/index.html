How to add a size chart on Shopify

I'm Tyler, and I make LeDuc Size Charts. The quick answer: you can put a size table on a Shopify page or in a product description by hand, or use an app to show a size chart pop-up on the product page. This page walks through both.

The built-in way

As far as I know, Shopify doesn't have a dedicated size chart feature. The usual built-in ways are:

  • Make a page (for example "Size guide"), put a table on it, and link to it from your product pages or menu.
  • Paste a table into the product description.

Both work. The downsides are that shoppers leave the product page to read a separate page, a description table has to be copied into every product, and neither gives shoppers a way to switch between inches and centimetres. If you only have one or two products, that may be fine.

With LeDuc Size Charts

  1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store.
  2. Click a template (women's tops, men's tops, pants, shoes or rings) and edit the numbers, or paste your own table straight from Excel or Google Sheets.
  3. Choose where the chart shows: specific products, collections, tags, vendors or your whole store. Save.
  4. On the app's home page, click Turn on automatic embed and save your theme. A "Size chart" link appears above Add to cart.
  5. Open a product page and click the link. The chart opens in a pop-up, and shoppers can switch between inches and centimetres with one tap. Ranges like 86-91 convert too.
  6. If several charts apply to one product, such as sizes and how to measure, they show as tabs in the same pop-up.
