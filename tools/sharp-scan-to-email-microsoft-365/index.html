Sharp scan to email and Microsoft 365

At the end of December 2026 Microsoft 365 stops accepting username-and-password email from copiers (SMTP AUTH with basic authentication). If your Sharp sends scans through smtp.office365.com with a password, it will stop. Here's how to check, and how to fix it.

Get Scan Relay, free 14-day trial Keeps your Sharp scanning to email without changing its firmware.

Is your Sharp affected?

In the copier's web page (its IP address in a browser), sign in as administrator and open System Settings, then Network Settings, then Services Settings, where the SMTP settings are.

If the SMTP server is smtp.office365.com (usually port 587) and the copier signs in with a Microsoft 365 email address and password, it's affected. Menu names vary by model; your dealer can also check over the phone. Or answer three quick questions and see.

What Sharp offers

Recent Sharp BP models can use OAuth with Microsoft 365, set up through an app registration in your Microsoft 365 tenant. Older MX models generally can't. Ask your dealer whether your exact model and firmware can do it: if it can, that's the cleanest fix.

If your Sharp can't use OAuth

  • Scan to a folder or OneDrive instead of email, if your staff don't mind the change.
  • A Microsoft 365 connector, if your office has a static public IP address and someone to set it up.
  • A local relay. Scan Relay, which I make, runs on any always-on office PC. You point the Sharp at that PC instead of smtp.office365.com, and it passes each scan on to Microsoft 365 with modern sign-in. No firmware update, static IP or DNS changes. Free for 14 days, then $79 once per office.

Setting up the Sharp for Scan Relay

  1. Install Scan Relay on a PC that stays on and sign in with the Microsoft 365 mailbox scans should come from.
  2. In the Sharp's email settings, set the SMTP server to that PC's address (Scan Relay shows it), port 25, no SSL/TLS. Leave any username and password; Scan Relay ignores them.
  3. Send a test scan.

Test before the end of December, while the old way still works as a fallback. More detail is in the full guide. Copier dealers and IT providers can buy wholesale licenses.
