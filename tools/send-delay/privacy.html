Send Delay for Outlook: privacy policy

Publisher: LeDuc Systems LLC. Last updated 2026-11-05.

What the add-in reads

When you press Send, Send Delay reads the recipient addresses of that message and any delivery time already set on it. It uses them only to decide whether to hold the message, inside Outlook, on your device.

What the add-in stores

Your rules (hold minutes, business hours, send-right-away addresses and domains) are saved in your own mailbox as Outlook add-in roaming settings. Nothing else is stored.

What leaves your mailbox

Nothing. Send Delay has no server that receives mail, addresses or settings. It sets the standard Exchange delivery time on the message, and Exchange Online holds and sends it. The add-in files (HTML, JavaScript, images) load from leducsystems.com like any web page; that web host may keep standard request logs (IP address, time, file requested).

Analytics and ads

None.
