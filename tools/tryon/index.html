Questions

How realistic is it?

The lipstick keeps her own lip texture, highlights and shading and takes the room's light, so a red reads as a red lipstick on her, not a sticker. Blush is a diffused flush, not a patch. Try the demo above with your own shades and judge it on your face.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android, live camera or a photo, rendered at the camera's own resolution. On a 2025 desktop it runs at about 60 frames a second; recent phones run it smoothly, older ones drop the resolution rather than the frame rate.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The camera frames stay on the shopper's device; the face is tracked and the makeup is drawn in her browser, and the tracker's model files are served from this site, not a third-party CDN. No camera frame, photo or face measurement ever leaves her device, nothing is stored, and there is no account for the shopper to create.

How do I add it to my store?

Paste one script tag into your product page template. A "Try it on" button appears under your Add to cart button, and tapping it opens the try-on over the page with your shades listed. You send me your shade list (name, colour, finish: matte, satin, gloss or sheer) and I load it. Any store page that can carry a script tag works, including Shopify and WooCommerce themes; I'll do the first one with you on a call.

What makeup does it do?

Lipstick and lip liner in four finishes, blush, bronzer, contour, highlighter, eyeshadow, eyeliner, mascara, brows, foundation and hair colour, as single products or as full looks.

Can I try it before paying?

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, and the demo above is free. Send me your shade list and I'll make you a demo link with your products loaded.
