Questions

How realistic is it?

The lipstick keeps her own lip texture, highlights and shading and takes the room's light, so a red reads as a red lipstick on her, not a sticker. Blush is a diffused flush, not a patch. Try the demo above with your own shades and judge it on your face.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android, live camera or a photo. The engine renders at the camera's own resolution at 30 to 60 frames a second on recent phones.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The camera frames stay on the shopper's device; the makeup is drawn in her browser. No camera frames or photos ever leave her device, and there is no account for the shopper to create.

How do I add it to my store?

Paste one script tag into your product page template and map each shade to its colour and finish (matte, satin, gloss or sheer). Shopify and WooCommerce themes both take it; I'll do the first one with you on a call if you like.

What makeup does it do?

Lipstick and lip liner in four finishes, blush, bronzer, contour, highlighter, eyeshadow, eyeliner, mascara, brows, foundation and hair colour, as single products or as full looks.

Can I try it before paying?

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, and the demo above is free. Send me your shade list and I'll make you a demo link with your products loaded.

What does it cost?

Starter is $49 a month for up to 25 shades on one store; Growth is $149 a month for unlimited shades. Cancel any time: email tyler@leducsystems.com and I'll cancel it the same day, with no charge if you're still in the trial.
