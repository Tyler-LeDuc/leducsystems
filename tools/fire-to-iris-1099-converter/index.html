What's changing

For years, information returns like 1099s could be e-filed through the IRS FIRE system as a fixed-width file in the Publication 1220 format. The IRS is retiring FIRE: its last filing day is November 19, 2026, at 3 p.m. Eastern, and after January 1, 2027, IRIS is the only IRS system for e-filing information returns, including corrections and prior years (irs.gov/fire, updated October 30, 2026).

A lot of accounting and payroll software only exports the Publication 1220 file. That leaves you with a file IRIS won't accept.

How it works

It runs in your web browser:

  1. Choose your Publication 1220 file, the one you would have uploaded to FIRE.
  2. It checks the file against its own totals, shows every form, and lists anything to check (name splits, missing state codes, foreign addresses).
  3. For an exact match, choose the blank template you downloaded from IRIS (Upload CSV with Form Data); its header row is used as-is.
  4. Download the IRIS CSV files: one form type and payer per file, split at 100 forms (IRIS takes up to 250 per CSV file, per IRS Publication 5717).
  5. You upload those files in the IRIS Taxpayer Portal and file.

It runs entirely in your browser. Your file, with its SSNs and TINs, is never uploaded to me or anyone else.

It converts 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, 1099-R, 1099-G, 1099-K, 1099-S, 1098 (mortgage interest), 1098-T (tuition) and W-2G for tax years 2022 and later. Need another form? Email me and I'll add it.

No FIRE file, just a spreadsheet of contractors and what you paid them? Use Spreadsheet to IRIS 1099-NEC; the same license covers it.

What it doesn't do

You still file through IRIS yourself. That needs an IRIS Transmitter Control Code (TCC) from the IRS, and getting one takes weeks. Apply for it now, whether or not you use this tool.

This tool, or a filing service?

Services such as TaxBandits and BoomTax take a FIRE file as it is and file it through IRIS for you, using their own TCC, for a fee per form. That's the easier choice if you file a few dozen forms once a year and don't want a TCC of your own.

This tool is for filers who have, or are getting, their own IRIS TCC and would rather not pay per form or hand their payees' SSNs to another company: accounting firms and payroll services filing for many clients, and organizations with a lot of forms. Why choose my IRIS template?

IRIS rejects a CSV whose header row doesn't match its template exactly, and the IRS only publishes the templates inside the portal. Choose the blank one you downloaded and its header row is used word for word. Without it, I use the column names from the IRS's published formatting guidelines.

What about corrected returns?

The IRIS CSV templates have no field for corrections, so corrected (G or C) records are set aside and listed. Make corrections in the IRIS portal itself.

Who isn't it for?

If you file only a handful of forms, you can type them straight into the IRIS Taxpayer Portal. If your software already files through IRIS or exports IRIS files, you don't need it. And it doesn't replace the IRIS TCC: you still need your own.

What happens to my file?

Nothing leaves your computer. The conversion and the checks run in your browser, so the SSNs and TINs in your file are never uploaded. Payment is handled by Stripe, which gives me your name and email. I never see your card.

Is this tax advice?

No. It converts the data already in your file into the IRIS format; it doesn't decide what to report. Check the preview, and the forms in IRIS before you submit. If you're unsure what belongs on a form, ask your tax professional.
