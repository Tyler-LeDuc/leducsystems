What's changing

For years, information returns like 1099s could be e-filed through the IRS FIRE system as a fixed-width file in the Publication 1220 format. The IRS retires FIRE on November 19, 2026. From the 2027 filing season, information returns are e-filed through IRIS.

A lot of accounting and payroll software only exports the Publication 1220 file. That leaves you with a file IRIS won't accept.

What I'm building

A tool that runs in your web browser:

  1. Choose your Publication 1220 file, the one you would have uploaded to FIRE.
  2. It checks the file for errors and converts it into the CSV upload templates for the IRIS Taxpayer Portal, split into as many files as IRIS's limits need.
  3. You upload those files in the IRIS Taxpayer Portal and file.

It runs entirely in your browser. Your file, with its SSNs and TINs, is never uploaded to me or anyone else.

What it doesn't do

You still file through IRIS yourself. That needs an IRIS Transmitter Control Code (TCC) from the IRS, and getting one takes weeks. Apply for it now, whether or not you use this tool.
