Does ChatGPT recommend your business?

More people now ask an AI assistant "who's the best med spa in Scottsdale?" and go with whatever it names. Type your business below and I'll ask ChatGPT real buyer questions for your category and city, then show you whether it names you, who it names instead, and which websites it got that from.

Free, no account, one check per business per week. I keep your email and the business details so I can answer questions about your result, and I may email you once about it. I don't sell or share them. Privacy

What the check does

I send ChatGPT a few questions a real customer would type, like "best {category} in {city}" and "who is the most recommended {category} in {city}?", through OpenAI's official API with web search turned on. Then I count:

  • how many of those answers name your business,
  • which other businesses get named instead, and how often,
  • which websites the answers cite: review sites, directories, articles, maybe yours.

If you give me your website, I also check the home page for the basics AI search reads: business schema, your city and category in plain words, a phone number, an FAQ, and whether your robots.txt blocks AI crawlers.

What this tells you, and what it doesn't

AI answers change from one run to the next, and the API isn't exactly the app on your phone. So treat one check as a snapshot, not a verdict. Name matching is automatic, so the full report shows every question and answer I counted. Nobody controls what an AI says, and I won't promise you'll be named. What I can do is measure it honestly.

The audit asks 50 buyer questions, lists every business named in your market and the sources the answers lean on, checks your website and listings, and gives you a fix list in order. Questions

Is this what I'd see in the ChatGPT app?

Close, but not identical. I use OpenAI's official API with web search turned on. Answers also vary from one run to the next, which is why I ask several questions and report the pattern, not one answer.

Which AI engines does the free check use?

ChatGPT, through the OpenAI API. If an answer fails during your check, the result says so and leaves it out of the count.

How long does it take?

Usually two to four minutes. Keep the page open; the result shows here when it is done, and the address of the page brings it back later.

Why only one free check per business a week?

Each check costs me real API money, and answers rarely change much in a week. If you need a re-run, email me.

What do you do with my email?

I keep it with the business details so I can answer questions about your result, and I may email you once about it. I do not sell or share it. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

Can you make ChatGPT recommend me?

Nobody controls what an AI says, and I won't promise you'll be named. What I can do is measure it honestly and give you a fix list for the things in your control: your website, your listings and the review sites the answers lean on.
