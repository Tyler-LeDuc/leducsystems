Questions

Which files open?

Access databases saved as .mdb (Access 97, 2000, 2002 and 2003) and as .accdb (Access 2007 and later, including Microsoft 365). If yours does not open, email me and I will look at it.

What does the viewer show?

Every table in the database: its rows, its column names and types, and the row count, with a search box that filters the rows. It does not show queries, forms, reports, macros or VBA code, and picture, attachment and OLE Object fields show up blank.

Is it really free to view?

Yes. Every table is free to view for any number of files. Only the exports cost money: $19 once unlocks Excel and CSV downloads for every database you open, or $7 for 24 hours. The license is a one-time payment, no subscription. Downloads keep working for as long as I run this service, and for at least 2 years from your purchase; if I ever shut it down, I'll email you 90 days' notice.

What is in the Excel export?

One sheet per table, named after the table, with the column names as a frozen header row and filters turned on. Numbers stay numbers, dates are real Excel dates and Yes/No fields become TRUE and FALSE. You can also export just the table you are looking at, as Excel or CSV. Before paying, you can download the first 20 rows of a table as a free sample.

What happens to my file?

It is sent over an encrypted connection to my server, read there by a program, and deleted as soon as it has been read, before the tables are shown to you. Nothing is stored and I do not open it. The license key is the only thing kept, in your own browser.

Does it work on a Mac?

Yes. Microsoft Access has never had a Mac version, which is why most people end up here. It works in Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox on Mac, Windows, Linux, Chromebook and iPad.

How big a database can it open?

Files up to 40 MB. For a very large table, the viewer loads up to 50,000 rows (fewer when the whole database holds a lot of data) and tells you when it has done that; search and exports then cover the rows loaded. If you need every row of a bigger table, email tyler@leducsystems.com.

Can I edit the data?

No. This is a viewer: it shows the tables as they were saved and exports them. To keep working with the data, open the Excel export, or import it into a database you can edit.

Do you have a license for a business or team?

Yes. The Business license is $99 for one year for up to 5 people at one business, and one key unlocks the exports in all six viewers: the MPP Viewer (Microsoft Project), the XER Viewer (Primavera P6), the Visio Viewer, the Access Viewer, and the MSG and PST viewers (Outlook). It is a one-time payment that does not renew, and checkout gives you an invoice for your records. Need a quote, W-9 or to pay by purchase order? Email tyler@leducsystems.com. If it doesn't work for your files within 30 days, I refund you in full.

What if my file does not open?

Databases with a password can't be read; remove the password in Access and save a copy first. If a file without a password fails, email tyler@leducsystems.com and I'll look at it. If an export doesn't work for your file within 30 days of buying, I refund you in full.

Is this made by Microsoft?

No. I'm an independent developer. Microsoft Access is a trademark of Microsoft, and this viewer isn't affiliated with Microsoft.
