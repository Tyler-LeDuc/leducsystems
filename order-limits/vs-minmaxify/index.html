LeDuc Order Limits vs MinMaxify

I'm Tyler, and I make LeDuc Order Limits. If you're looking at MinMaxify and wondering whether there's a cheaper or simpler option, here is how the two compare. I've kept it to what my app actually does and what MinMaxify says on its own App Store listing.

Both apps set minimum and maximum quantities and use Shopify's checkout validation to enforce them. The differences are mostly in price, in which features sit on which plan, and in a few things MinMaxify does that mine doesn't.

What MinMaxify does that mine doesn't

  • Per-variant limits. Their listing includes variant-specific rules. Mine counts all of a product's variants together; I can't set a different limit on just the large size.
  • Multilingual messages on their Premium plan. My messages are editable, but in one language.
  • Checkout UI extensions on their Plus plan. My app doesn't add anything to the checkout page beyond the validation messages Shopify shows.
  • More places to show alerts. Their listing names cart alerts, checkout alerts, product page alerts, pop-ups and custom messages. Mine shows the checkout messages plus an optional note on product pages; there are no pop-ups.

Mine also doesn't do per-customer limits over time (my limits apply per order), country or market limits, or limits that switch on and off by date. If you need any of those, my app isn't the right one.

When mine is the better fit

  • You want a flat, low price. One paid plan at $7.99/month gives unlimited rules and every feature I have. Whole-order limits and two rules are free, with no time limit.
  • You want customer-tag rules and collection totals on one plan. Each rule can apply to everyone, only customers with certain tags, or everyone except those tags. Collection totals cap or require a total number of items across the collections you pick. Both are on the same plan as everything else.
  • You need Buy it now and express checkout covered without a higher tier. My limits are enforced by Shopify's Cart and Checkout Validation on every plan, so Buy it now, express checkout buttons and edited carts can't skip them.
  • You sell in packs or cases. "Sold in multiples of" works per product, collection, tag, vendor or all products, alongside the minimum and maximum.

Other things it does: whole-order minimum or maximum items, subtotal and weight; messages you can reword; an optional product-page note that also sets the quantity box limits; and a switch on the home page to pause enforcement.

How to switch

There's usually no way to export limits from one app and import them into another, so switching is done by hand. It goes quickly:

  1. List your current limits. Open MinMaxify and write down each limit: what it applies to, the minimum, maximum or multiple, and any customer tags or collections it uses.
  2. Recreate them as rules in my app. Each one takes a minute or two. Customer-tag targeting and collection totals map straight across, and whole-order limits (items, subtotal, weight) go in the Order limits page.
  3. Test a cart. Add a quantity that should be blocked and go to checkout. You should see your message and be unable to continue. Then fix the quantity and check that checkout goes through.
  4. Turn off or uninstall MinMaxify. Do this once you're happy, so two apps aren't blocking checkout at the same time with different messages.
