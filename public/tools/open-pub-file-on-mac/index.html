Questions

Why won't my Mac open a .pub file?

Microsoft only ever made Publisher for Windows. Preview, Pages, Keynote, and Word and PowerPoint for Mac can't read the .pub format, so double-clicking one does nothing useful.

Do I need to install anything?

No. This page works in Safari, Chrome or Firefox on any Mac. The conversion runs on my server, so there is no app to install and no Windows needed.

Which Mac app should I edit it in?

Keynote is already on your Mac and opens the .pptx file, one slide per page. PowerPoint for Mac opens it too. Affinity, which is free for Mac, opens the .idml file as a full layout document with linked columns. Canva imports the .pptx in your browser.

What about LibreOffice or a Windows virtual machine?

Both work. LibreOffice is free for Mac and its Draw app opens most .pub files, though larger layouts can shift. Running Windows in Parallels with an old copy of Publisher is the most faithful, but it means buying Windows and finding a copy of Publisher, which Microsoft no longer sells.

Why did my .pub files stop opening?

Microsoft retired Publisher. It was removed from Microsoft 365 in October 2026 and support for the standalone versions ended the same month. Word and PowerPoint can't open .pub files, so without Publisher they no longer open at all.

Is the PDF free?

The preview of every page is free, no account needed. The clean PDF, with the page pictures, is $4.99 per file, and it's included free with the editable versions ($12) or the $19 pass.

Every page, text box, picture, shape, line and table comes across as an object you can edit. You see a preview of every page before you pay.

Which should I use?

If you have Microsoft 365, PowerPoint is the easiest: it's already installed and works well for flyers, newsletters, cards and brochures. Canva is good if you'd rather work in your browser. Affinity (free from Canva) and InDesign are full layout programs and keep articles linked from column to column.

How do I open it in Canva?

In Canva, choose Upload and pick the .pptx file. Canva imports it as an editable design.

How do I open it in Affinity or InDesign?

Choose File > Open and pick the .idml file. It opens as a normal document you can edit and save.

What about fonts?

Your text keeps the fonts it used in Publisher. If the computer you open it on doesn't have one of them, the app will substitute another, and text can reflow slightly.

Which Publisher versions work?

Publisher 98 through Publisher 365. Files from Publisher 95 and earlier, and password-protected files, aren't supported.

What happens to my file?

Your file is converted on my server, used only to make your download, and deleted automatically within 24 hours, or within 7 days once paid, so you can download it again. I never look at it or share it.
