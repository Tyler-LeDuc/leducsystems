Questions

Which files open?

Documents made in Apple Pages (.pages), from Pages 5 and later (2013 to today) and from the older Pages '09. Numbers spreadsheets (.numbers) open in my Numbers Viewer; Keynote presentations (.key) are not supported yet.

Is it free to view?

Yes. Every page is shown free, for any number of documents, with a light "Preview" mark across it. Only the downloads cost money: $19 once unlocks the Word file (.docx) and a clean PDF for every Pages document you open, or $7 for 24 hours. The license is a one-time payment, no subscription. Downloads keep working for as long as I run this service, and for at least 2 years from your purchase; if I ever shut it down, I'll email you 90 days' notice.

Will the Word file look exactly like the original?

Close, but not always identical. The document is converted by LibreOffice, an open source program that reads Pages files. Text, headings, lists, tables, pictures, headers, footers and footnotes come through. Fonts Apple uses that Windows does not have are replaced with similar ones, and complex layouts (text wrapped around shapes, unusual table styles) can shift. The preview shows you exactly what the download looks like before you pay.

Are there free ways to open a .pages file?

Yes, and they may be all you need. If you have an Apple ID, Pages runs free in a browser at icloud.com. You can also ask the person who sent it to use File, Export To, Word or PDF in Pages. This viewer is for when neither is handy: no account, no waiting on the sender.

What happens to my file?

It is sent over an encrypted connection to my server, converted there by a program, and deleted before the pages, the PDF and the Word file are sent back to you. Nothing is stored and I do not open it. The license key is the only thing kept, in your own browser.

How many pages?

The first 30 pages are shown as pictures in the browser. The Word file and the PDF have every page. Files up to 40 MB.

Can I install it?

Yes. In Chrome or Edge, use the install button in the address bar. On Windows and ChromeOS the installed app can then open .pages files you double-click or choose with "Open with".

What if my file does not open?

Password-protected or damaged documents can't be read. If a file fails, email tyler@leducsystems.com and I'll look at it. If a download doesn't work for your file within 30 days of buying, I refund you in full.

Is this made by Apple?

No. I'm an independent developer and this viewer isn't affiliated with Apple. Pages is a trademark of Apple Inc.
