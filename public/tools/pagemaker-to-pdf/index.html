Questions

Why won't my PageMaker files open?

Adobe discontinued PageMaker in 2004. InDesign CS6 and earlier came with a PageMaker converter, but newer InDesign versions dropped it, and PageMaker itself no longer runs on current Windows or macOS.

Is the PDF really free?

Yes. Every file you drop in gets a free PDF, no account and no card. You only pay $19 if you want the editable versions.

Which PageMaker versions work?

PageMaker 6.0, 6.5 and 7.0 (.pmd, .p65, .pm6 and template files). Files from PageMaker 5 and earlier aren't supported yet. If yours doesn't convert, the preview tells you before you pay anything.

What about pictures?

PageMaker often linked pictures instead of storing them in the file. Pictures stored in the file come across; linked ones that aren't inside the file can't, so you'd place those again in your new app.

What about fonts?

Your text keeps the font names it used in PageMaker. Many old fonts aren't on today's computers, so your app substitutes a similar one and some text may reflow. Headlines in unusual fonts are the most likely to need a touch-up.
