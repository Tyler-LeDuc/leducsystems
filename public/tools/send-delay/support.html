Send Delay for Outlook: help

Holds every message you send for a few minutes, like the classic Outlook "defer delivery" rule, in new Outlook for Windows and Outlook on the web.

Where is my held message?

In Drafts. Exchange sends it at the time shown, usually within a minute or two, even if Outlook is closed. Open it in Drafts to edit it. To send it now, open the Send Delay pane, choose No delay, then press Send.

Turn on the automatic hold for everyone (admins)

  1. Open the Microsoft 365 admin center, then Settings > Integrated apps.
  2. Choose Get apps (or Upload custom apps with the manifest file), search for Send Delay, and deploy it to the users or groups you pick.
  3. Accept the request to run when a message is sent. It can take up to 24 hours to reach every mailbox.

Installed by a single user from Get Add-ins, Send Delay works per message: open a message, choose Delay this message on the ribbon, then press Send.

Rules

  • Hold for: 1 to 120 minutes after you press Send.
  • Business hours: a message that would go out after hours waits for the next start time.
  • Send right away: when every recipient is in your own domain, or when any recipient matches an address or domain on your list.

Works with

Exchange Online (Microsoft 365) mailboxes in new Outlook for Windows, Outlook on the web, classic Outlook for Windows and Outlook for Mac. Not available on Outlook mobile, POP or IMAP accounts. In classic Outlook for Windows a held message does not show in Outbox or Drafts and can't be edited after Send; it still sends at the set time.
