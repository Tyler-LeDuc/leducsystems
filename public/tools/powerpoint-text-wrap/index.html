Wrap text around pictures in PowerPoint

PowerPoint has no text wrap, so a picture on a text box just covers the words. Drop a .pptx and get it back with the text flowing around your pictures, the way Publisher or Word would do it. It's still normal, editable PowerPoint text.

One payment per file, no subscription. Files where no picture covers any text aren't charged. It's a one-time layout: after big text edits, upload the file again.

Questions

How does it work?

PowerPoint can't wrap, but it can show several text boxes. For each text box with a picture over its words, the tool measures your text with the real fonts and splits it into boxes above, beside and below the picture, the way Publisher would flow it. The text stays real text with its own font, size, colour and bullets.

What do I see before paying?

A before-and-after picture of every slide it changed (up to 12), so you can check the result. Files where no picture covers any text are not charged: the tool tells you there is nothing to fix.

What if I edit the text later?

PowerPoint doesn't reflow text between boxes, so after a big edit the lines won't meet up any more. Upload the file again (or fix small edits by hand). The original layout is easy to undo: the pictures are untouched and the text is all there.

What does it leave alone?

Text a picture doesn't cover, text with animations, text set in the middle or at the bottom of its box, rotated text, and any box where the wrapped text would have to shrink below 70% of its size or run into other text. Those stay exactly as they were.

Will it look the same on my computer?

The text is measured with standard fonts that match Calibri, Cambria, Arial, Times New Roman and Georgia exactly. With other fonts, line breaks can come out slightly different, so check the result and nudge a box if a line spills.

Does it work with Google Slides, Keynote or Canva?

Upload a .pptx: PowerPoint saves one, and Google Slides, Keynote and Canva can all download one. The result opens in all of them, since it is just text boxes and pictures.

What happens to my file?

It is processed on my server only to make your download, and deleted automatically (after a day, or 7 days once paid). I never look at it or share it.

What if it doesn't work for my file?

Email tyler@leducsystems.com within 30 days and I'll refund you.
