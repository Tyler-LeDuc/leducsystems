1099 deadlines for 2027 (tax year 2026)

The 1099s for payments you made in 2026 are due in early 2027. January 31 falls on a Sunday and February 15 is Presidents' Day, so several dates move. Here they are in one table, with what else changes this season: the $2,000 threshold for 1099-NEC and IRIS replacing FIRE.

The due dates

FormCopy to the recipientFile with the IRS
1099-NECFebruary 1, 2027February 1, 2027 (paper or e-file)
1099-MISCFebruary 1, 2027 (February 16 if you report in box 8 or 10)March 1 on paper, March 31 e-filed
1099-INT, 1099-DIV, 1099-R, 1099-G, 1099-KFebruary 1, 2027March 1 on paper, March 31 e-filed
1099-B, 1099-SFebruary 16, 2027March 1 on paper, March 31 e-filed
W-2 (to the SSA, not the IRS)February 1, 2027February 1, 2027

The usual dates are January 31, February 15, February 28 and March 31. When a due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, the IRS treats a return as on time if it's filed or furnished by the next business day (General Instructions for Certain Information Returns). That rule turns January 31, 2027 (a Sunday) into February 1, February 15 (Presidents' Day) into February 16, and February 28 (a Sunday) into March 1. Check the instructions for your exact form before you rely on a date.

Form 8809 can get you more time to file with the IRS for most forms, but not to send recipients their copies, and an extension for 1099-NEC is only granted for specific hardship reasons.

What's different this season

  • $2,000 for 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC. For payments made after December 31, 2025, the reporting threshold for most nonemployee compensation and many 1099-MISC payments rose from $600 to $2,000. Check the current instructions for the form and box you're filing.
  • FIRE is gone. The IRS FIRE system takes its last filings on November 19, 2026. Returns for tax year 2026 are e-filed through IRIS, which takes forms typed into the portal or uploaded as the IRS's own CSV templates. More in the IRS FIRE system is closing.
  • You need an IRIS TCC. A FIRE TCC doesn't work in IRIS, and the IRS can take weeks to approve an application, so apply now if you'll file yourself. Already applied? See what to do while your IRIS TCC is pending.
  • 10 or more returns means e-filing. Since 2024, filing 10 or more information returns in a calendar year (most types counted together) generally means you have to e-file.
