Tempo cost calculator

Enter the number of users on your Jira Cloud site. That is the only thing this page asks for, and nothing you type leaves your browser.


Which prices this uses

Tempo: the published Atlassian Marketplace price list for "Timesheets by Tempo - Jira Time Tracking", Cloud, the standard per-user subscription that every Jira Cloud site buys through the Marketplace. Read on 3 November 2026; Tempo last changed it on 13 October 2026. It does not include Tempo's separate add-on apps (Tempo Planner, Cost Tracker and others), and it does not know about any discount you may have negotiated.

Saguaro Hours: free for up to 10 users, then $2.00 per user for users 1 to 100, $1.50 for users 101 to 250 and $1.00 per user from 251.

How the total is worked out: above 10 users, each band is charged at its own rate (graduated): for 250 users, Tempo is 100 x $5.99 + 150 x $4.21 = $1,230.50 a month, Saguaro Hours is 100 x $2.00 + 150 x $1.50 = $425.00. Annual subscriptions cost 10 times the monthly price. This matches the totals Atlassian publishes for both apps. Atlassian bills every user on your Jira license, so use your Jira user count, not the number of people who log time.

Sources: Tempo's Marketplace price list, https://marketplace.atlassian.com/rest/2/addons/is.origo.jira.tempo-plugin/pricing/cloud/live , and Tempo's pricing page, https://www.tempo.io/pricing/jira-time-tracking . How Marketplace billing works: Jira time tracking app pricing.

Prices can change. Check the sources before you decide.

What you get for the difference

Saguaro Hours covers weekly timesheets on Jira's own worklogs, billable flags and custom attributes, teams with a lead, submit and approve, reports with CSV and Excel export and a dashboard gadget. It does not have start and stop timers, capacity planning, cost tracking or a public API. The full list, including what Tempo has that Saguaro Hours does not: Tempo alternative.
