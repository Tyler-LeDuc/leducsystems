Tempo Timesheets pricing in 2026

This page sets out what Timesheets by Tempo costs on Jira Cloud, using Tempo's public price list. It also covers who counts as a user, what Tempo's other apps add to the bill, and what cheaper options cost. I make Saguaro Hours, one of those options, so read the comparison with that in mind.

All prices are monthly list prices in US dollars for a whole Jira Cloud site, read on 2026-11-04.

Who counts as a user

Every user on the Jira license, not only the people who log time. Tempo's pricing page says: "A user is anyone who can log into Jira" and "Your Tempo license size must match your Jira license size." This is how Atlassian bills all paid Marketplace apps on Jira Cloud, so it applies to every app on this page. More on the rules: how Jira app pricing works.

So the price per person who actually tracks time can be much higher than the list rate. A 100-user site where 20 people log time pays $599.00 / 20 = $29.95 a month for each of them.

Annual subscriptions

Atlassian prices an annual cloud subscription at 10 times the monthly price, bought for a user tier. Tempo's pricing page has an Annual toggle but showed no annual prices when I read it. Details and sources: how Jira app pricing works.

Tempo's other apps are billed separately

Timesheets records and approves time. Cost tracking and capacity planning are separate Tempo apps, each with its own price for every Jira user:

100 $599.00 $318.00 $439.00 $1,356.00

Rates behind the table: Financial Manager is $10.50 flat up to 10 users, then $3.18 for users 1 to 100 and $1.91 for 101 to 250. Capacity Planner is $11.00 flat up to 10 users, then $4.39 and $2.64. Sources: https://marketplace.atlassian.com/rest/2/addons/io.tempo.cost-tracker/pricing/cloud/live and https://marketplace.atlassian.com/rest/2/addons/com.tempoplugin.tempo-planner/pricing/cloud/live (both last changed 2026-10-13). Tempo's pricing page notes Financial Manager is "Included with Tempo Budgets", with no bundle price shown.
