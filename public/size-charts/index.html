LeDuc Size Charts

Size chart pop-ups for Shopify product pages, with an inches/centimetres switch. One flat price that's never billed by your orders or traffic.

Set up in three minutes

  1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store.
  2. Click a template (women's tops, men's tops, pants, shoes, rings) and edit the numbers, or paste your own table from Excel or Google Sheets.
  3. Choose where it shows: specific products, collections, tags, vendors or all products. Save.
  4. On the app's home page, click Turn on automatic embed and save your theme. A "Size chart" link appears above Add to cart.

FAQ

What does it cost?
Free for 2 size charts. Pro is a flat monthly price for unlimited charts, with a free trial. It's never billed by your order count, page views or anything else that grows with your store.
Can shoppers switch units?
Yes. Enter your chart in inches or centimetres and shoppers can switch between them. Ranges like 86–91 convert too. Charts like ring or shoe sizes can be set to "no units".
Can one product show more than one chart?
Yes. If several charts apply, the pop-up shows them as tabs, for example "Tops" and "How to measure".
Does it slow my store?
No. The script is small, loads after the page, and only runs on products that have a chart. It's a theme app extension, so there are no code edits and it removes cleanly.
