LeDuc Restock Alerts

Back in stock emails for Shopify. Shoppers ask to be told when a sold-out item returns, and the app emails them the moment it does. One flat price, with no per-email fees.

Set up in two minutes

  1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store.
  2. On the app's home page, click Turn it on, then save your theme. A "Notify me when available" button now appears under Add to cart whenever the chosen variant is sold out.
  3. Optional: in Email settings, set the sender name, reply-to address and subject line.

FAQ

How fast do alerts go out?
Within seconds of a restock. Shopify notifies the app when inventory changes, and every sold-out item with shoppers waiting is also re-checked every 10 minutes, so a missed update delays an alert instead of losing it. The app's home page shows when stock was last checked.
What does it cost?
Free for up to 50 alerts a month. Pro is $9.99 a month for unlimited alerts, with a 14-day free trial. There are no per-email fees or overage charges.
Who is the email from?
It shows your store name as the sender, and shopper replies go to your store's contact email (or any address you choose).
Does it work with my theme?
It's a theme app extension, so there are no code edits and it removes cleanly. It's tested on Dawn and Horizon, and you can change its text and colors in the theme editor under App embeds.
What if a product sells out again?
Shoppers get one email per sign-up. If they miss it, they can sign up again.
