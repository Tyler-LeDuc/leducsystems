LeDuc Sold Out Sorter

Sold-out products move to the bottom of your collections automatically, and go back to their old spot when they're in stock again.

Set up in one minute

  1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store.
  2. Manually sorted collections are sorted right away. For collections sorted another way (best selling, newest), click Switch to manual and sort. Their current order is kept as the starting point.
  3. That's it. Products move as soon as they sell out or come back, and every collection is re-checked every 30 minutes.

FAQ

What counts as sold out?
A product whose variants can't be bought: inventory is tracked and at zero or below. Products set to keep selling when out of stock stay where they are.
Where does a restocked product go?
Back to the position it had before it sold out, not the end of the list.
Why only manually sorted collections?
Shopify only lets apps reorder manually sorted collections. Shoppers who choose their own sort on the storefront, like price, see that sort instead.
Can I leave a collection alone?
Yes. Turn sorting off for any collection on the app's home page.
